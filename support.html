Tip: if something goes wrong, tell us your Apple Watch and iPhone models, their software versions and the app version (iPhone: Profile → About).

Frequently asked questions

How do I start a workout?

On Apple Watch, open Watch Your Fit and tap Program. Choose one of your programs, Free workout, or Outdoor for a walk, run or ride with GPS. Programs are created on iPhone in the Programs tab and appear on the watch automatically.

My workouts don’t appear on iPhone.

Workouts move to iPhone automatically when the watch and the phone are connected. Keep them close to each other and open the app on iPhone. Connection status is shown on iPhone in Profile → Apple Watch.

The watch shows heart rate but no zone.

Zones need your maximum heart rate. On iPhone, open Profile and set your date of birth or enter the maximum heart rate manually. Also check that the app is allowed to read heart rate: Settings → Privacy & Security → Health → Watch Your Fit.

How does the subscription work?

New subscribers get a free trial, then a monthly or yearly subscription. The price and trial length are shown on the subscription screen before you buy. The subscription renews automatically unless you cancel it at least 24 hours before the end of the current period. Without a subscription your history stays available to view, but new workouts can’t be started on the watch.

How do I cancel or restore the subscription?

Cancel in Settings → your name → Subscriptions on iPhone, or in the app: Profile → Manage Subscription. To restore it on a new device, open the subscription screen and tap Restore Purchases. Purchases are tied to your Apple Account, so use the same one.

Where is my data? What happens if I get a new iPhone?

Your data is stored only on your Apple Watch and iPhone — we have no servers. Workouts from the watch are kept on iPhone too, and iPhone data is included in your iCloud or computer backup. Restore the new iPhone from that backup to keep your history.

How do I delete my data?

Delete the app from Apple Watch and iPhone. Workouts saved to Health remain there; delete them in the Health app: your profile → Apps → Watch Your Fit. Deleting the app doesn’t cancel the subscription. Details are in the Privacy Policy.

Health notice

This app is a workout log, not a medical device. Heart rate, heart rate zones, calories and estimated 1RM are for reference only and are not suitable for diagnosis or treatment. Consult a doctor before you start training and if you feel unwell.
